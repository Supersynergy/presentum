The Single-Source Document

Why HTML beats PDF as the authoring layer · SuperSynergy Research

PDF is an export. The source should be HTML — readable, searchable, themeable, and printable to pixel-perfect pages on demand.

The thesis

For a decade, teams authored in one tool and exported to another. The result: drift, dead links, and image-dump PDFs no one can search.1 In 2026 the browser is the most capable layout engine on earth — and it ships everywhere.

One file. Looks great on screen. Prints to a perfect PDF. Sent directly.

The evidence

Across ten conversion methods, image-based exporters strip searchable text and break accessibility, while dead engines carry security risk.2 Browser-grade rendering wins on fidelity; native print-CSS wins on control.

Conclusion

Author once in HTML. Let the reader choose: read it live, or print it to PDF. The artifact becomes a true single source of truth — and you stop maintaining two copies of everything.
